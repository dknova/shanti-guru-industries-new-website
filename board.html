How governance works

What each body does

A general description of the role these bodies usually play in a listed company.

The Board

Sets strategy, oversees management and is accountable to shareholders.

Audit Committee

Reviews financial reporting, internal controls and the work of auditors.

Nomination and Remuneration

Considers board appointments and the pay framework for directors and senior management.

Stakeholders Relationship

Looks into shareholder and investor grievances and service standards.

Risk Management

Identifies business risks and reviews the steps taken to manage them.

Independent directors

Bring an outside view and help protect the interests of minority shareholders.
