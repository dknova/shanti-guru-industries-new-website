Each document appears on its page as a PDF card that opens the file.
To publish a document: put the PDF in the matching folder below (replacing the placeholder with the same name keeps the page working as is),
or add a new line to the page's DOCPAGE list, e.g. {t:'Title',m:'Details',u:'documents/policies/policies/file.pdf',s:'Source'}.
Folders: meetings/{general-meeting-minutes,voting-results,extraordinary-general-meetings}, conduct,
policies/{policies,independent-directors-appointment-letters,independent-directors-policy,familiarization-programs}.

Financial > Annual Reports: PDFs are in documents/financial/annual-reports/ and are listed in the CAT array at the top of financial.html
(each entry is [year, filename-without-.pdf]).
Financial > Half Yearly Results: PDFs are in documents/financial/half-yearly-results/ and are listed in the CAT array in financial.html (HY base path).

Financial > Related Party Transactions: local PDFs are in documents/financial/related-party/ and listed in the CAT array in financial.html.

Meetings > General Meeting Minutes: AGM PDFs are in documents/meetings/general-meeting-minutes/ and listed in the DOCPAGE config in meetings.html.

Meetings > Voting Results: PDFs are in documents/meetings/voting-results/ and listed in the DOCPAGE config in meetings.html.

Shareholding > Shareholding Pattern: local PDFs are in documents/shareholding/shareholding-pattern/ and listed in the CAT array in shareholding.html (LOCAL map for recent quarters, OLD list for earlier ones).
Shareholding > Share Capital Reconciliation Audit Report: local PDFs are in documents/shareholding/share-capital-reconciliation/ and listed in shareholding.html (LOCAL2 map for recent quarters, OLD2 list for earlier ones).
