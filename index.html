About Us

A Company Built on Business Experience and Growth

Shanti Guru Industries Limited, formerly known as RCL Retail Limited, is an Indian company incorporated in Tamil Nadu in 2010. With a focus on food products trading, the company aims to develop its business through customer satisfaction, market understanding and sustainable business practices. Our journey reflects our commitment to creating value for stakeholders while continuously exploring opportunities in the food products sector.
