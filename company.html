About Us

A Company Built on Business Experience and Growth

Shanti Guru Industries Limited, formerly known as RCL Retail Limited, is an Indian company incorporated in Tamil Nadu in 2010. With a focus on food products trading, the company aims to develop its business through customer satisfaction, market understanding and sustainable business practices. Our journey reflects our commitment to creating value for stakeholders while continuously exploring opportunities in the food products sector.

What we do

Retail, trading and distribution

Our stated objects centre on exclusive retail outlets for agro-based food products, and our current line of business is trading in food products.

Retail outlets

Exclusive outlets for agro-based food products and everyday packaged goods, close to the customer.

Food products trading

Sourcing and supplying food products and fast moving consumer goods with consistency and care.

Brands and own products

We distribute brands across South India and make selected products under our own name.

How it works

From sourcing to shelf

An illustrative view of how a product travels through our trading model.

  • Sourcing

    We choose products and suppliers based on demand, quality and reliability of supply.

  • Quality checks

    Goods are reviewed for packaging, labelling and consistency before they move on.

  • Storage and planning

    Stock is planned against demand so that popular lines stay available.

  • Distribution

    Trade partners across South India receive supply through our network.

  • Retail

    Products reach customers through retail outlets and partner stores.

  • Feedback

    Customer and trade feedback shapes what we stock and make next.
